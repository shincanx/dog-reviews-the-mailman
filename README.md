One funny one-line review a dog gives the mailman — 159 characters, in the dog's own voice: he sounds the alarm, the mailman runs, and the dog gets paid in naps.

> He shows up at noon, I sound the alarm, he shoves the goods through the slot and runs. Five stars — easiest security job I've ever had, and I get paid in naps.

`main.py` is deterministic, uses no network and no dependencies. It writes that one line to `entry.txt` in the output directory (`MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR`, else `/output`).
